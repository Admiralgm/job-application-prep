# Humanizer Mandate (2026-05-24)

**ACTIVE — all user-facing output must be humanized.**

The user explicitly requested on 2026-05-24: "use humanizer skill to humanize all future responses."

This means:
- EVERY piece of prose the user sees must pass through the humanizer skill
- CVs, cover letters, application form answers, evaluation reports, email drafts — all of it
- No exceptions for "internal" or "working" text — if the user reads it, it's humanized

## What to strip
- AI vocabulary: "delve," "tapestry," "landscape" (abstract), "pivotal," "showcase," "underscore," "foster," "leverage," "paradigm," "holistic," "synergy"
- Formal discourse markers: "Furthermore," "Additionally," "Moreover," "In addition," "Notably," "Importantly," "As a result," "Consequently," "Therefore," "Thus," "In conclusion," "To summarize"
- Perfect paragraph symmetry — vary length abruptly
- Generic conclusions — "The future looks bright," "Exciting times lie ahead," "I hope this helps"
- Chatbot artifacts — "Great question!," "Of course!," "Certain!," "Let me know if...," "Here is a..."
- Three-item lists forced into "Rule of Three" — use 2 or 4 when natural
- Hyphenated word pair overuse — "third-party," "cross-functional," "data-driven," "decision-making"
- Excessive hedging — "it could potentially possibly be argued that"
- Filler phrases — "In order to" → "To," "Due to the fact that" → "Because"

## What to add
- First-person grounding — "When I worked in...," "A colleague told me..."
- Genuine uncertainty — "I'm less certain about this part," "This seems right to me but I could be wrong"
- Opinions — "I genuinely don't know how to feel about this" beats neutral pros/cons
- Specificity — name companies, numbers, dates instead of vague claims
- Sentence length variation — short sentences next to long ones
- Abrupt transitions sometimes — just jump to the next point

## How to apply
1. Write the first draft normally (content-first)
2. Re-read through the humanizer lens
3. Strip AI patterns, add human ones
4. Read aloud — if it sounds like a press release, keep editing
5. Target: the prose should sound like the person who did the work is telling you about it, not a model summarizing it
